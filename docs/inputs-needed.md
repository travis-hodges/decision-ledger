# Inputs needed from the team

The first four inputs unlock a credible proposal and live product verification:

1. **Applicant and route:** legal entity, UEI, SAM registration status, and whether this is an SBIR or STTR application. For STTR, identify the research institution and work split.
2. **Two demonstration cases:** one point trade and one decision that changed over time. Supply authorized unclassified source records, requirements, alternatives, costs, and outcomes. Redacted or anonymized material is fine if provenance remains reviewable.
3. **Reviewers:** an engineering or acquisition domain lead, a data/source reviewer, and the person who can approve use of those cases. Identify any Army transition or customer contact we may cite with permission.
4. **Model access:** set `OPENAI_API_KEY` in the repo's local `.env` and tell us it is ready. Do not paste the key into chat or commit `.env`.

Then confirm the intended business entity name and product name, current eligibility and solicitation instructions in DSIP, proposal lead, budget/team capacity, any existing past performance, target deployment boundary, and which historical pricing sources the team is authorized to use. We can proceed with UI, architecture, and evaluation work while these are collected.
